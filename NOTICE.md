## Derived terrain data (Mielon's The Sift, MIT)

The files under `src/main/resources/sift_dimension_datapack/data/thesift/worldgen/`
`noise/`, `density_function/`, `carver/`, `noise_settings/the_sift.json`, and the
`biome_source` table in `dimension/the_sift.json` were generated by
`scripts/port_mod_terrain.py` from the datapack of the NeoForge port of
Mielon's The Sift (Copyright (c) 2026 Mielon, MIT License). Only the namespace and
the custom terrain block IDs were rewritten. The MIT copyright and permission
notice must be kept with these files; do not drop it when a final NOTICE/LICENSE
is written. This supersedes the earlier "no attribution claim" statement for
those paths only.

## Ported gameplay logic and features (Mielon's The Sift, MIT)

Beyond the terrain data above, several gameplay behaviours were re-implemented
by reading the reference mod's source/datapack (Copyright (c) 2026 Mielon, MIT
License): the Singer barter reward table (`SingerBarter`), the Soul Block /
Ancient City portal frame scanner (`PortalFrameScanner`) that detects an
arranged frame ring to open the gate, the portal song/console note-sequence
logic (`PortalSongTracker`), mob stat lines (Echo Golem health, Blub taming),
advancement trigger mapping, and ore/feature placement data derived in commit
"port ore features" (all per the MIT attribution requirement — the copyright
and permission notice must be kept with any final NOTICE/LICENSE). These are
clean-room-style ports of observable behaviour plus direct datapack JSON reuse
where noted; they carry the same MIT obligation as the terrain files.

## Ported worldgen features and portal structures (Mielon's The Sift, MIT)

The dimension datapack's additional `worldgen/feature/` and
`worldgen/placed_feature/` JSON files (sculk blobs and sprouts, siftslate
stalks and hanging roots, overgrown vegetation patches, the ichor underground
lake), the biome `carvers` wiring, the biome `features` stage layout, the
`worldgen/structure/` JSONs, `worldgen/structure_set/` and
`worldgen/template_pool/` files, the eleven portal `worldgen/structure/*.nbt`
structure templates, and the `tags/block/` predicate tags were generated by
the port scripts (`scripts/port_mod_terrain.py`, `scripts/port_mod_ores.py`) from the datapack of Mielon's The Sift
(Copyright (c) 2026 Mielon, MIT License). The namespace was rewritten
(`the_sift:` to `thesift:`) and custom blocks/fluids were replaced with the
vanilla stand-ins documented in `integrations/content-spec.json`; the mod's
custom Java feature types are not ported (the plugin generates that content
as vanilla stand-ins through `SiftEcologyManager`). The MIT copyright and
permission notice must be kept with these files.

## NeoForge Port Reference (MikiKox1)

The worldgen data used for porting terrain features was cross-referenced with
the unofficial NeoForge port by MikiKox1. The NeoForge port's worldgen data has
been sampled (8 files) and matches the original Fabric mod, except for biome
JSONs which were modified in the port. Credit to MikiKox1 for the NeoForge
port effort.

## Removed plugin-original content

The earlier plugin-original additions were removed from the runtime, catalog,
provider definitions, datapack, tests, and packaged assets. This includes the
Spire Tyrant/Prism Warden branches, Rift Key and gate items, fan-made discs,
Blub Meat, duplicate terrain families, extra biomes, and other stand-in mobs.
They must not be reintroduced or described as parity with Mielon's mod.

## Verified third-party material included

The MIT-licensed data and behavioural ports from Mielon's The Sift listed in
the sections above are verified inclusions, and the four Sculkflower textures
are verified byte-for-byte copies. Everything else attributed to third-party
contributors (ImpSteve, Fuzja Jądrowa, Echo_Dev, StrawberrySushi_) is NOT
included; do not treat reference research as proof that anything beyond the
listed files was copied or licensed.

## Current content scope

`integrations/content-spec.json` is the authoritative mod-only catalog. It
contains only the reference mobs, items, blocks, and the ten reference biomes
that this Paper port can represent with provider-backed content or documented
vanilla stand-ins. Legacy IDs from earlier plugin iterations are not supported.
